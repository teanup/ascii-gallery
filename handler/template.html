Usage

View an animation in your terminal:

curl {{.BaseURL}}/anim/{id}

Upload from the command line:

curl -F "file=@animation.gif" {{.BaseURL}}/anim

Upload with custom dimensions:

curl -F "file=@animation.gif" -F "width=120" -F "height=40" {{.BaseURL}}/anim
